    How this works

      Results are practice estimates. They are not official IELTS results, and this site is not affiliated with IELTS, the British Council or IDP.

      Writing mock test

      Exam conditions. The real Writing paper lasts 60 minutes: Task 1 about 20 minutes (at least 150 words) and Task 2 about 40 minutes (at least 250 words). In Exam mode the clock cannot be paused, there are no hints, and time up locks your answer.

      The combined 60-minute Writing Mock is available from the dedicated Mock Tests tab. It includes both tasks, one continuous timer and one combined report.

      Use Practice Studio here for individual Task 1 and Task 2 practice.

      Listening mock test

      Original WritSpeak practice material with computer-generated voices. Each test has 3 parts and 15 questions. You hear each recording once, as in the real test, so use headphones and a quiet room. A short pause at the start of each part lets you read the questions.

      This is a short mock (15 questions). An IELTS band needs a full 40-question test, so you get a raw score here.
